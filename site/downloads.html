Geometry

Commune shapes are Thiessen (Voronoi) cells around one seed point per commune, clipped to the national outline. They are approximations for mapping and neighbour relations, not administrative boundaries. 1,497 map units: the 41 arrondissements are merged into 6 cities and 6 communes have no seed point yet.
